WHY NUDGEGOO?

Not another list.
Just the right moment to remember.

Making a detailed checklist every time you leave can be a burden. What you need may already be in your memory or in the notes you use every day.

NudgeGoo does not create another task. It gives you a small cue: “This is the moment to remember.”

Save home, work, or another familiar place. NudgeGoo gently notifies you as you leave—only when the moment matters.

WHAT IS NUDGEGOO?

A small nudge for every departure.

NudgeGoo is not a to-do app or a packing list. It helps you recall what matters at the moment you leave.

Core features are free Save places, receive departure alerts, check, and be seen off for free.

01

A gentle alert as you leave

Save home, work, a supermarket, or another place you visit often. Turn reminders on or off and choose a radius for each place.

02

One short question after the alert

Open the notification and your character asks if you checked your things. It is a brief cue to recall what you need—not another list to complete.

03

A quiet send-off when you are ready

Once you have checked, your character sends you on your way with a short message. No tracking of your answer, and no extra steps.

04

Choose from six characters

The stick figure is free. Five more characters are available as one-time purchases, each with a different tone for alerts and send-offs.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Help when you need it.

Check these common questions about settings and everyday use.

I am not receiving notifications

In your device settings, make sure location access for NudgeGoo is set to “Always” and notifications are allowed. Alerts may be delayed by signal conditions or the state of your device.

How is my location handled?

Location is processed only on your device and is not sent to the developer's servers. See the privacy policy for details.

How do I restore purchases?

Use “Restore Purchases” on the character selection screen.

What changes when I buy a character?

The character's appearance and the tone of notification and send-off messages change. Each character is a one-time purchase.

Can I use NudgeGoo for free?

Yes. Saving places, departure alerts, checks, and send-offs are all free. The free stick figure has the same core functionality.

What notification radius should I use?

Start with the default 50 m. A wider radius means you need to travel farther before leaving the area, so the alert may feel later.
