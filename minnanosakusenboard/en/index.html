WHY I MADE IT

Rediscovering
the joy of soccer.

When my children and family started playing soccer, I rediscovered how much I enjoyed the game.

I played soccer in elementary school, but I also remember feeling discouraged by the gap between myself and more skilled players.

That is why I want today’s young players to experience not only the joy of improving, but also the joy of thinking about the game.

I created Minnano Sakusen Board to give teams more opportunities to move ideas around, talk through them, and try them together.
